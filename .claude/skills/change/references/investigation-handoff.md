# Investigation handoff

The packet is the dev document Build executes. Scale detail to behavior and
risk, not length.

For a v2 binding, read the latest retained intent, requirements, answers, corrections, and
experiment limitations. Preserve the digest binding and map every active
material user requirement in `investigationCoverage` to an actual Change
requirement/scenario, non-goal, or design decision. Do not promote agent
proposals, superseded revisions, or silence into requirements. A missing map is
agent-owned drafting work, not repeated approval. Keep mappings valid through
revise and amend; a new handoff during Build belongs in the amendment with
updated coverage.

For a detailed-document request, preserve before/after behavior, examples,
exceptions, constraints, acceptance criteria, decisions, rejected alternatives,
and experiment limits in native packet fields. Use bug reproductions and
expected behavior, API contracts and errors, data migration/compatibility, or
workflow states and recovery where relevant. Do not invent answers or add
approval steps to lengthen the packet. Do not fill irrelevant sections or change
the assurance lane merely to make documents longer.

After compilation, inspect source-to-target coverage and the retained-context
appendix. Confirm examples, criteria, answers, corrections, and limitations
survived rendering, and compare meaning with each source; links alone do not
prove semantic equivalence. Repair missing detail through draft, revise, or
amend, then rerun invalidated checks.
