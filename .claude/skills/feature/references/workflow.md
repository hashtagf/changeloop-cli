# Feature workflow

Invoke `grill-task-gu`; read PRD/backlog and code/tests. Settle production,
wire, activation, proof, and delivery authority before one Decision Sheet.
Only `--schedule` asks dates. Choose one group.

Compile one semantic draft through Change and obtain compiled-spec approval;
the PRD Decision Sheet does not replace it. Then use `advance --through proven`
for isolated Build and Prove. Do not construct packet/dispatch/proof command
chains manually. The configured harness policy owns reviewer selection,
independence, dispatch rounds, timeouts, and closure. Aggregate findings and
repair the returned dependency-ordered batch; resume only invalidated checks.
When risk-tiered review is required, its policy summary is: Low risk uses one full AI
review; Medium permits one correction wave and one fresh-session delta closure; High
asks material risk decisions in the initial Decision Sheet. After one
closure review of a repaired final delta, never dispatch another AI from this
skill. Follow the harness's typed infrastructure recovery and supported
decisions. These review-wave limits do not limit product repair attempts.
Product repair has no fixed retry count while progress changes. The harness
owns tool readiness and permission integration; do not send routine recovery
commands to the user. A real authority, resource, conflict, budget, or repeated
no-progress boundary preserves state and returns supported choices.

`--resume` continues. Let the coordinator reuse identity-valid receipts and proof;
never reuse stale or out-of-scope evidence. Never Land, publish, or weaken evidence.
Report progress and proven outcome in the user's language.
